# Assets

**Art direction:** Asphalt Opera — a neo-noir urban poster treatment with wet asphalt, concrete, sparse sodium-vapor light, Signal Orange for hostile presence and Forge Lime `#C6F13D` for objective and progress. The world remains tangible and street-level; it is not a purple cyberpunk skyline.
