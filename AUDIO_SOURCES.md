# StreetForge Audio Sources

## Background ambience

StreetForge uses the non-MIDI file `dungeon_ambient_1_0.ogg` by **JaggedStone** as a quiet, optional world ambience layer. The source page labels the work as **CC0** and describes it as a loopable cave/dungeon ambience with low-frequency wind and water drips. The track is loaded only after a player gesture and can be muted at any time.

| Asset | Role | License | Source | File URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Loopable Dungeon Ambience | Low-volume environmental ambience for a live district | CC0 | [OpenGameArt – JaggedStone](https://opengameart.org/content/loopable-dungeon-ambience) | https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/dungeon_ambient_1_0.ogg |
| Punch SFX pack, clip 03 | Short action response for confirmed melee actions | CC0; credited in project records | [OpenGameArt – qubodup](https://opengameart.org/content/punch) | https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/qubodupPunch.7z |

## Source selection policy

Sound effects are limited to independently documented CC0, public-domain, or equally explicit royalty-free assets. MIDI is not used. Browser audio stays opt-in because autoplay restrictions and player accessibility take precedence over ambience.
